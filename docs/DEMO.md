# Demo script (3–4 minutes)

Phone with Expo Go, location on, on CityU Wi-Fi or mobile data. Profile → Diagnostics → **Test backend**
first (every function should say OK). The day before: see SETUP.md → "The day before a demo".

## 1. Walking, our core (60 s)

1. Map → search **Mong Man-wai Building** → Route here (from Kowloon Tong Exit A1).
2. The **Best now** tab follows the weather. Profile → Diagnostics → Simulate weather → **Rain**: the route
   switches to the most covered one (the card says how much of it is covered). **Heat**: the coolest one.
3. Tap a route → the explanation and the "estimated" labels: we never claim more than the data shows.

## 2. Compare every way to travel (60 s)

1. Same trip, tab **All**: walk, car, taxi side by side — time, cost, CO₂, walking minutes, labels.
2. New trip **CityU → Mong Kok**: the MTR card shows the line colour, the Octopus fare and
   "Kwun Tong Line from Kowloon Tong at 08:23 · then 08:27 (live)" — real MTR Next Train data.
3. Taxi card: "HK$37–42 (meter)" from the official tariff. Sort chips: Cheapest, Greenest…

## 3. The nudge (45 s)

1. Profile → Diagnostics → **Simulate rush hour**. Back to Exit A1 → CityU: the car is now stuck; walking
   gets **Fastest** and the green nudge "As quick as driving · saves ~386 g CO₂". The banner says it's
   simulated — at 6 pm this is real.
2. Select the MTR option: "Leave in 4 min for the 08:30 KTL", and the phone buzzes when it's time.

## 4. Live city (30 s)

1. Layers → **Transit stops** → tap the Festival Walk minibus stop: next arrivals, live or scheduled.
2. Layers → **Traffic** and **Incidents** (Transport Department traffic news).

## 5. Community (30 s)

Friends → a friend sharing location → "Getting there: best is MTR, 12 min". Record a route → it appears for
everyone, and the computed routes learn from walkers' feedback.

**If the network fails:** walking routes for the demo trips and the five demo multimodal trips come from saved
answers, labelled "saved for the demo". Turn both simulations off afterwards (they reset on app restart).
